Sabaic Roots

The Old South Arabian Language of Saba

Saba was a kingdom in what is now Yemen, on the southwest corner of Arabia. Its people wrote Sabaic on stone from roughly the eighth century BCE into the early centuries of the common era. This course covers the kingdom of Saba, its script, its gods, and the real, documented connections between Saba and ancient Kemet. Travel with Mia, Malik, and Nana.
